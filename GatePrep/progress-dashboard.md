# GATE 2027 progress dashboard

A topic is not mastered because the notes were read. In this dashboard, **Mastered** stays `no` until all seven boxes for that row are checked.

Class A, B, or C changes how long the row takes. It does not change the seven boxes, and it does not drop the row. Every official syllabus id from `syllabus-checklist.md` is here: 176 rows.

## Mastery boxes

| Box | Check it only when |
| --- | --- |
| Theory | You can state the definition, the standard method, and the usual trap without reading the notes. |
| Basic | The basic-problem quota for that class is solved with full working. |
| GATE-level | The GATE-level quota is solved with full working. Numerical answers show each calculation step. |
| PYQ | Relevant verified previous-year questions, up to the class cap, are solved unchanged. Year, paper, marks, and official key are recorded. |
| Mistakes | Every miss in that topic is written in the mistake log: what you did, why it was wrong, and the correction. |
| Revision | The formula and concept sheet for that topic has been revised the required number of times. |
| Closed-book | A fresh closed-book set is solved at least one day later, without notes. |

**Mastered** is `yes` only when Theory, Basic, GATE-level, PYQ, Mistakes, Revision, and Closed-book are all checked.

PYQ box, when no official question exists for that exact row: record `none verified` after the papers are checked, and say which years were searched. Do not check the box with an invented or rewritten question. An original GATE-style problem fills the GATE-level quota. It does not become a PYQ.

## Practice and revision quota

The quota is the amount of work that earns the box. It is not a suggestion to stop early on an A topic, and it is not permission to skip a C topic.

| Class | Meaning | Basic | GATE-level | Verified PYQ cap | Advanced | Revision passes | Closed-book set |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | Must master | 6 | 6 | 8 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| B | Important | 3 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| C | Supporting, still official | 2 | 2 | 2 | 0 | 1 | 2 |

Revision timing:

- A: about 2 days after the first pass, again at the end of that subject, and again in the last 20 days.
- B: at the end of that subject, and again in the last 20 days.
- C: once, in the last 20 days. The first pass still includes its basic and GATE-level problems.
