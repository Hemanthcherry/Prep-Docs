## Paper pattern that bounds the 90-day plan

Official pattern for CS (not a syllabus topic):

- Computer-based, English, 3 hours.
- 10 General Aptitude questions + 55 subject questions = 65 questions.
- General Aptitude: 15 marks. Subject section: 85 marks. Total: 100.
- Inside those 85 subject marks, Engineering Mathematics is 13 marks and is CS-specific. The other subject questions are 72 marks.
- Each question is 1 mark or 2 marks.
- From 10 questions worth 15 marks, GA is exactly five 1-mark and five 2-mark questions.
- From 55 questions worth 85 marks, the subject section is exactly twenty-five 1-mark and thirty 2-mark questions. The official page does not further split the 13 mathematics marks into a question count.
- Wrong MCQ: −1/3 for a 1-mark question, −2/3 for a 2-mark question.
- MSQ and NAT: no negative marking. MSQ: no partial credit.
- Abilities tested: recall, comprehension, application, analysis and synthesis.

## How to read each row

- **Official** — words taken from the GATE 2027 PDF. These are the only items that must be taught as syllabus.
- **Label** — `official`. Supporting prerequisites and optional advanced notes are not rows in this checklist. Add them in the study text only when a problem needs them, and mark them in the text as supporting or optional.
- **Class** — `A` must master, `B` important, `C` supporting. Time and practice only.
- **Covered** — `no` until a study section exists. Covered is not the same as mastered.

Atomic rows split an official list. They do not add topics the PDF does not name.

## What must stay out of the “official syllabus” label

These are absent from the GATE 2027 CS and GA PDFs. They may appear only with an explicit label.

- **Supporting prerequisite** — only the minimum needed to solve an official topic. Examples that are not themselves official rows: C syntax needed for PDS.1, datapath detail needed to design an ALU, a grammar fact needed before parsing.
- **Optional advanced** — standard textbook topics the 2027 PDF does not name. Examples from the 2026 comparison and from common coaching lists: secondary storage, OSI-versus-TCP/IP as a model question, framing, Ethernet bridging, flooding, ARP, DHCP, ICMP, UDP, SMTP, FTP, Email, NP-completeness, AVL trees as a required PDS topic.

A 2026 PYQ that tests a removed networks or COA line can still be used as a verified past question. The write-up must say the tested line is not in the GATE 2027 syllabus, and it must not be counted as coverage of a 2027 row.

## Dependency order for the later 90-day schedule

These links are preparation order, not extra syllabus rows.

- C programming → data structures → algorithms
- Discrete mathematics → algorithms → theory of computation
- Digital logic → computer organization
- Theory of computation → compiler design
- Operating systems ↔ computer organization
- Databases ↔ algorithms
- Networks ↔ operating systems
- Engineering mathematics is used inside algorithms, networks performance, OS, and databases; it should not be left until after those subjects
- General Aptitude is independent and is 15 of 100 marks, so it belongs in the weekly plan from the start rather than in a last-week block

Numerical practice, when writing starts, is heavier on Engineering Mathematics, Digital Logic, COA, Algorithms, OS, DBMS, and Computer Networks. Each numerical solution needs the full calculation, not only the final value.

## PYQ rule already in force

No previous-year question is included in this checklist. When study sections are written, a question may be called a PYQ only with a verifiable year, paper, official key, and unchanged statement. Anything else is labeled “Original GATE-style practice question.”
